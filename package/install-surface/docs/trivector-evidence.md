# Standalone routing evidence and diagnostic selection

The pure routing modules accept explicit bounded evidence and time. They read no environment, private files, database or provider state. `normalizeTrivectorEvidencePacket` detaches and freezes closed packets; `selectTrivectorCandidate` proposes a head with fixed diagnostic reasons. Neither function authenticates observation origins or grants execution, capacity, lease or effect authority. Semantic acceptance remains null.

Binary gate observations and active matching limitations are evaluated before fit or pins. Resolved capability incompatibility gates; conflicts, unknown values and structured-output null stay explicit. The fit producer owns the declared task score and its capability weighting. This packet supplies task-class context, without phase/lane/effort admission.

Legacy arbitration uses only optional fresh stamped `legacy_quota.remaining_fraction`; absent evidence stays unknown. It never derives a legacy fraction from request units. v4.1 uses fresh quota windows normalized by each estimated request cost and applies its existing 429 hazard adjustment. Unknown, stale, future and reset-expired quota is flagged and omitted. A reset does not fabricate replenishment. Blocking window exhaustion is a v4.1 head gate. Legacy and v4.1 G9 numerical policies remain separately explicit and fingerprinted; transport/process failures include timeouts and exclude separately recorded 429 events.

Half-open circuit context carries `CIRCUIT_PROBE_REQUIRED`. A proposal does not prove probe eligibility or reserve a probe. An owner must verify the actual exclusive reservation before any dispatch. This head-only model does not admit fallback seats.

Provider/domain floors use the supplied symbolic identities and provenance claims. Unknown domains contribute zero. The model does not authenticate independence or resolve aliases through a live registry. A plant adapter owns canonical identity acquisition and verified provenance. Full normalized packet, injected time and selection policy enter decision digests, including uncertainty; digests establish consistency, not authenticated origin.

Remaining: thin source-pinned owner adapters, exact account and quota observations, authenticated grants, atomic claims and reservations, accepted-result lineage, shadow calibration, installed canaries and each plant's independent rollback/release proof. This source layer changes no live router default or weights.

## Operational join assessment

The source validator already bounds every array to32 entries, including 429 history, before selection. Binary gates precede the fit bucket, pin and quota/latency/static arbitration. This selection is lexicographic rather than a blended-weight score, so these modules do not establish weight normalization or calibration. Decisions remain diagnostic proposals with execution, capacity, lease and effect authority false.

Operational acceptance still requires:

- Independently authenticated owner receipts binding gate, quota-window/reset, inflight-cost and failure evidence to the exact account and attempt; hashes and source labels establish context consistency only.
- Task-feature and capability evidence bound to the admitted task/phase/lane/effort; the supplied fit scalar and uncertainty flags do not provide that join.
- Accepted-result lineage and representative replay/shadow evaluation for reliability, latency and cost; transport/process success counters do not establish semantic success or calibration.
- Owner-proven independent failure domains and admitted diversity floors; symbolic provenance labels cannot authenticate independence.
- Native atomic capacity/lease reservations and causal attempt, retry/cancellation and terminal-result correlation before execution, followed by installed canary and plant-specific release evidence.

These are missing evidence joins, not a proposal to alter weights or invent a new scoring policy. Standalone product, optional personal integration, company authority and each plant keep separate ownership. Historical deferred growth or app plans do not authorize activation; Constellation remains permanently retired while generic projections and headless contracts continue.
