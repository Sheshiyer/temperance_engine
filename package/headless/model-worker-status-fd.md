# Status/release endpoint adapter, source and mocks

`model-worker-status-fd.py` adapts internally owned owner-side endpoints to the reviewed `StoppedProtocol` interface. Child protocol roles are fixed FD5 (status writer) and FD6 (release reader). Owner counterpart descriptor numbers may differ; this helper never duplicates, remaps, or overwrites unrelated parent descriptors and has no CLI/environment selector.

The caller supplies a closed trusted expected owner/first-child context before status intake. The independently observed stopped-process token must match that retained context before release. This is cooperative owner provenance, not authentication. The original FD3/4 launch codec ACK/EOF and reservation join is still missing; the retained ACK callback is only a trusted assertion.

The two transferred endpoints require distinct FIFO identities and read/write access modes respectively. First identity discovery is independent for both peers, including admission failures. Original identities and initial flags remain retained. Nonblocking and close-on-exec flags use check/set/recheck; FD-table exclusion and atomic close-on-exec are not claimed. Every ordinary operation checks the original retained monotonic deadline before and after; synchronous syscalls and callbacks cannot be preempted. Initial remaining lifetime is at most 120 seconds, never renewed.

Status reads are zero-wait, at most 64 bytes per call; EAGAIN returns pending. The reviewed outer parser enforces its 484-byte frame, closed schema and bounded step count. Status EOF closes its owner reader before independent native-stop observation. The outer parser remains responsible for complete framing and resource checks.

The caller deliberately injects the reviewed control encoder. There is no implicit source loader or assertion that an imported closure is bounded. The adapter verifies exact encoder bytes against the supplied 69-byte control frame, verifies the pipe atomic bound, and attempts one write. The returned count is not a child readiness ACK. Partial writes, EAGAIN and uncertainty never authorize another write or payload replay. Writer closure precedes a fresh retained-token observation and the injected continuation callback. Continuation attempts are sticky before invoking the callback; uncertain returns or exceptions hold and cannot be retried. Identity matching in this adapter alone is not resource-policy admission. The outer stopped protocol validates fresh pressure/RSS/physical/descendant evidence; this adapter does not implement native signals or resource enforcement.

Adapter teardown has at most two original endpoint identity queries and two close attempts, independently attempted and marked before errors; additional outer protocol checks are separate. Replacement identities are not closed. Failed closure remains unknown and cannot regain success. The caller must invoke teardown on outer protocol cancellation/hold as well as normal completion. Child cleanup/reap and actual private-channel writer ownership remain future joins.

The 13 inert fixtures inject all syscall and callback seams and integrate the actual published stopped protocol. They establish source behavior only: no actual FD, child, native observation, signal, provider or model is invoked. No Build policy, paid capacity, execution authority, actual reservation, containment or native adapter acceptance follows.
